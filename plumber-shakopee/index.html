Shakopee, MN 55379: Just Across the River

Shakopee is the Scott County seat, with 43,698 residents in 2020 and about 15,378 housing units. It lies across the Minnesota River from Chaska, reached by Highway 41, with US Highway 169 and County Highway 101 serving the rest of the city.

When Highway 41 is closed for river flooding, travel between Chaska and Shakopee changes. Tell us when you call and we plan the route.

Sewage Connection to Chaska and the Blue Lake Plant

Wastewater from Chaska flows through a Metropolitan Council lift station to the Blue Lake Wastewater Treatment Facility in Shakopee. Plumbing across both cities ends at the same plant.

We have not confirmed Shakopee's water utility or hardness, so ask your utility how your address is served. Growth accelerated after the 1996 realignment of Highway 169.

Plumbing in Shakopee follows the Minnesota Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and commercial work is done by licensed Minnesota contractors.

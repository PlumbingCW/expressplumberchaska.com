What to Do for an Overflowing or Leaking Toilet

Turn the shutoff valve at the wall clockwise until it stops. If it does not move, lift the float in the tank so the fill valve cannot open, and shut the main water valve to the house if water keeps coming.

Do not flush again, even if the bowl seems to be draining. A partial blockage is easy to turn into a flood.

Problems We See Most Often

Running toilet

A worn flapper, a stuck float or a fill valve that never shuts off. A running toilet can waste dozens of gallons a day.

Water at the base

A failed wax ring or loose closet bolts. Leaks here can rot the subfloor.

Weak flush

Clogged rim jets, a low tank water level or a partial drain blockage.

Rocking bowl

Loose bolts or a damaged flange. It breaks the seal and lets sewer gas and water escape.

Repeated clogs

A partial blockage in the trap or the line, or an older low-flow toilet that no longer clears well.

Toilet Repair and Replacement Cost

Small internal repairs such as a flapper or fill valve commonly run from about $100 to $250 in typical US pricing. A reset with a new wax ring costs a little more, and a new toilet installed commonly runs from about $300 to $800 depending on the model. Price depends on:

PartsStandard parts are inexpensive. Specialty or comfort-height models cost more.
Floor conditionRotted subfloor from a long-term leak adds repair work.
FlangeA broken or low flange needs a repair ring.
Water-saving modelPressure-assisted or high-efficiency models differ in price and install.

Water damage from a sudden overflow may be covered by homeowners insurance. A slow leak that has gone on for months often is not.

When a New Toilet Makes More Sense

A toilet that cracks, leaks from the bowl, clogs weekly despite a clear line or needs frequent parts is cheaper to replace. Modern high-efficiency toilets flush strongly on far less water.

If a toilet clogs repeatedly and the line is the cause, sewer line cleaning solves it better than replacing the bowl.

Permits and Water Use in Chaska

The City of Chaska requires a plumbing permit for installing or replacing plumbing fixtures. A homeowner can pull a permit for a toilet replacement in an owner-occupied primary residence, and we pull permits for the work we do when required.

A running toilet also shows on your water bill. The City's four-day consumption alert and its two-hour meter test help you spot leaks like this one early.

A faucet that will not shut off, or a supply line spraying under a sink, needs the angle stop closed right away. Turn the small oval handles under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, shut the main water valve.

What to Do When Water Sprays From a Faucet or Valve

Close the shutoff valve below the sink. If a supply line is leaking, place a bucket under the connection and dry the cabinet floor. Water that sits in a cabinet softens particleboard and starts mold.

If the shutoff valve itself leaks or will not close, the main water valve is the next stop. Call us and tell us which one is failing.

Why Faucets Drip

Worn cartridge or washer

The most common cause. The part wears and no longer seals.

Corroded valve seat

Hard water and age pit the seat, and a new washer will not stop the drip.

Loose packing or O-ring

Leaks around the handle or spout base.

Failed supply line

Braided lines can fail at the crimp and drip into the cabinet.

Shower and Tub Faucets, and Stuck Shutoffs

A shower that drips from the spout after you shut it off almost always needs a new cartridge or stem. Replacing it is usually an access-panel job, not a wall demolition.

A shutoff valve that will not close is a repair waiting to become a flood. We replace stiff multi-turn stops with quarter-turn valves during the same visit, so your next repair does not start with a scramble for the main.

Faucet Repair Cost

A cartridge or washer repair commonly runs from about $100 to $250 in typical US pricing, and a faucet replacement from about $200 to $500 depending on the fixture. What changes it:

Faucet brandPremium brands cost more in parts.
Valve conditionA corroded shutoff valve must be replaced along with the faucet.
AccessA tight cabinet or a tub deck takes longer.
Fixture you chooseA replacement faucet you supply is cheaper than one we supply.

A sudden supply line burst that damages a cabinet may be covered by homeowners insurance. A slow drip is generally a maintenance issue.

Hard Water Wears Faucets Faster

The City of Chaska's drinking water is hard, at 26 grains per gallon in the 2025 report. Minerals build up in aerators, around cartridges and on valve seats, and they shorten the life of faucet parts.

Cleaning the aerator and, if you can, adding a water softener reduces how often you see a drip.

Permit and Shutoff Notes for Chaska

Fixture replacement, including a faucet, requires a plumbing permit in Chaska. Homeowners can pull a permit for work in an owner-occupied primary residence, and we handle the permit for the jobs we do.

Know where your main water shutoff is and test it once a year. It is the fastest fix for any leak.

Replacement faucets and shutoffs are installed to the Minnesota Plumbing Code, which follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), including backflow protection on hose connections.

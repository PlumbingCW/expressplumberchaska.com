Repiping in Chaska, MN

Repeated pinhole leaks, rusty water, falling pressure and pipes that corrode from the inside are the signs that a plumbing system has reached the end of its life. Repairing one leak at a time costs more over the years than replacing the failing pipe.

We repipe Chaska homes in PEX or copper, whole house or in sections, with clear scopes, drywall repair coordination and permit handling.

Signs Your Pipes Need Replacing

Repeat pinhole leaks

A second or third pinhole in the same system means other sections are thin.

Rusty or brown water

Galvanized steel rusts from the inside and sheds iron.

Low pressure at several fixtures

Mineral and rust buildup narrows old pipe.

Visible corrosion

Green crust on copper or flaking orange on galvanized.

Water stains on ceilings

Slow leaks hidden in walls.

PEX or Copper: How We Choose

PEX is flexible, resists freeze damage better than rigid pipe and can be run through walls with fewer openings, which keeps drywall repair small. Copper is long-lasting and has no plastic in it, which some owners prefer.

We explain the tradeoffs for your house, and we use whichever the home and budget suit. Both meet the Minnesota Plumbing Code.

What a Repipe Week Looks Like

On day one we set up protection, shut the water at the main and begin running new lines from the manifold or main. Fixtures are connected one room at a time so water is restored each evening wherever possible.

The final step is a pressure test and inspection. Drywall openings are patched or handed to your drywall contractor, and we leave the system labeled so you know where each shutoff goes.

Repiping Cost

Whole-home repiping commonly runs from about $4,000 to $15,000 or more in typical US pricing, depending on the size and access. Partial repipes cost less. The price depends on:

House size and fixturesMore bathrooms and floors mean more pipe.
AccessOpen basement ceilings are far cheaper than finished walls.
Pipe materialPEX is usually less expensive than copper.
Drywall repairPatching and painting are coordinated and priced as needed.

Insurance rarely pays to replace pipes that are wearing out. It may cover water damage from a sudden burst.

Hard Water, Permits and Repiping in Chaska

The City's 2025 report lists Chaska water at 26 grains per gallon, so scale is a factor in older systems. The City also reports lead service line inventory results as of late 2025, with no lead lines found and 1,169 services of unknown material still being verified.

Repiping requires a plumbing permit and inspection in Chaska, and we handle both. If you want to check a service line, the City points to a Lead Inventory Tracking Tool at maps.umn.edu/LSL.

New piping is installed under the Minnesota Plumbing Code, which follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and is pressure tested and inspected before walls are closed.

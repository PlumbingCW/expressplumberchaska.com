Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Chaska, MN

When the basement floor drain gurgles and the toilet upstairs will not flush, the blockage is in the main line. Stop running water, do not flush again, and call. The longer water sits in a full pipe, the more likely it is to find the lowest opening in the house.

We clear main sewer lines in Chaska with drum machines and heavy cutters, then follow with a camera when the line has a history, so you learn why it blocked and how likely it is to happen again.

Main Line Backup: What to Do Right Now

Stop all water use, including the washing machine and dishwasher. Close the lids on toilets and cover floor drains with a rubber mat or board if you must, since they are where water will rise.

Then call. We want to know where the cleanout is, how many fixtures are slow and when it started.

How We Clear a Main Sewer Line

1

Find access

A main cleanout outside or in the basement is the best entry. If there is none, we pull a toilet or use a roof vent.

2

Cut through

A drum machine feeds a heavy cable and a cutter head through the blockage, tackling roots, paper and grease.

3

Clear the debris

We flush the line and watch for the water to run freely at the lowest drain.

4

Confirm

If the line has a history, a camera pass shows what remains and what caused the clog.

What Causes Main Line Clogs

Tree roots

Roots enter joints looking for water and trap paper. Mature trees along older streets are common culprits.

Flushed wipes

Wipes labeled flushable often do not break down and snag in the line.

Grease

Kitchen grease cools and coats the pipe, growing until flow stops.

Pipe defects

A belly, offset joint or collapsed section holds water and catches solids.

Cleanout, City Main and Your Responsibility in Chaska

The City of Chaska treats the sewer service line from your property to the City main as the property owner's responsibility. A cleanout on that line is the best place to start any clearing.

If a backup affects several homes on your street, call the City's Water and Sewer Department at 952-448-4335. The City main flows to a Metropolitan Council lift station and on to the Blue Lake plant in Shakopee.

Cleanouts and the sewer service line are governed by the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) that Minnesota's plumbing code follows, which is why a cleanout is the right place to start.

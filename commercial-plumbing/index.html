Plumbing Emergency at Your Business? Start Here

Find the nearest shutoff for the affected fixture or area and close it. If water is spreading and you cannot stop it, close the building's main. Keep the area clear of customers and staff, and shut off power to equipment standing in water if you can reach the breaker safely.

Take photos of the damage for insurance and call us with what you see. We give you a clear price before we begin.

Backflow Testing and Compliance

The City of Chaska requires backflow prevention assemblies to be tested after installation and then annually by a certified tester. A failed assembly can send contaminated water back into the public supply.

We repair or replace assemblies that fail a test, and we coordinate the paperwork so you keep your records current.

Commercial Plumbing Pricing

Commercial work is quoted on scope. A restroom repair is priced like a residential repair, while a grease line, backflow replacement or water heater swap is priced after we see the system. The main variables:

Building size and systemMore fixtures and longer lines mean longer jobs.
Hours of workWorking after close or overnight to avoid disrupting business may affect cost.
EquipmentCommercial heaters, assemblies and interceptors cost more than residential equivalents.
CompliancePermits and test reports are part of the job.

Business interruption and property policies can respond to sudden plumbing damage. Photograph the loss and keep invoices.

Commercial Permits and Testing in Chaska

The City of Chaska allows homeowners to pull a plumbing permit only for repairs in their own owner-occupied home. Commercial and new construction plumbing is done by licensed Minnesota contractors.

Backflow assemblies must be tested after installation and every year by an ASSE-certified tester, per the City. You can look up a contractor's license on the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry website.

Commercial work follows the Minnesota Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), including backflow protection, and licensed contractors do the permitted work.

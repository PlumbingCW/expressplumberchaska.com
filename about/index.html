Who We Are

A Chaska Plumbing Company With a Phone That Gets Answered

Chaska Express Plumbing is based at 706 N Walnut St in Chaska, Minnesota. We handle emergency and planned plumbing for homes and small businesses in Chaska, Chanhassen, Victoria, Carver, Waconia and Shakopee.

Our line is open 24/7. A person answers, asks what is happening and tells you what to do first, whether that is closing a valve, stopping water use or keeping people away from an electrical panel.

No pressure on the phone

How We Handle Emergencies

A plumbing emergency is stressful, and the first few minutes matter more than the arrival. Our first job on the phone is to help you stop the damage: which valve to close, what to unplug, what to keep away from water.

After that, we dispatch from Chaska and tell you what to expect. When a plumber arrives, they look at the problem, explain it in plain terms and give you a price before any work starts.

Standards and Local Rules

Plumbing in Minnesota follows Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, the state plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code. The City of Chaska requires a plumbing permit for installation, alteration, repair or addition of plumbing, including water heater and water softener replacement.

Homeowners may pull permits for work in their own owner-occupied primary residence, and licensed Minnesota contractors handle other work. Inspections are scheduled through the City, and we coordinate them for the jobs we do.

What You Can Expect When a Plumber Arrives

Expect a plumber who listens first, looks at the problem and explains it in words you can follow. You will hear what is wrong, what the options are and what each costs before any work begins.

If a repair is enough, we say so. If a replacement is the smarter choice, we show you why, and we leave the final decision with you. When the work is done, we test it, clean up and explain what to watch for.

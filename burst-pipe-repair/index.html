Frozen Pipe, Not Yet Burst?

If a faucet gives only a trickle in a cold snap, a pipe is likely frozen. Open the tap, then warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or heating pad, starting at the faucet end. Never use a torch or open flame.

If you cannot find the frozen section or see any bulge or leak, call. A frozen pipe that thaws can leak where it split.

Burst Pipe Repair Cost

A simple burst pipe repair commonly runs from about $200 to $800 in typical US pricing, with water damage cleanup priced separately. Larger or multiple breaks cost more. The price depends on:

Number of breaksOne split is quick. A long freeze can split several sections.
LocationAn exposed basement pipe versus one inside a finished wall.
Pipe typeCopper, PEX and galvanized each repair differently.
After hoursNights and holidays may carry a surcharge, stated up front.

Homeowners policies commonly cover sudden pipe bursts and resulting water damage, though they may exclude damage from neglect, such as heating a house inadequately while away. Call your insurer promptly.

Minnesota Freezes and Chaska Homes

Minnesota winters regularly bring multi-day deep cold. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages, crawl spaces and below-grade water entries are most at risk. A water service line entering a basement wall also freezes if the room is unheated.

Know where your main shutoff sits and keep the house heated to at least 55 degrees when away. For a service line outside the house, see water line repair.

Replacement pipe and fittings go in under the Minnesota Plumbing Code, which follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and only approved materials are used for permanent repairs.

A Planned Community Inside Chaska

Jonathan was started by the Jonathan Development Corporation in 1967 and named for explorer Jonathan Carver. It was planned as a town on woods, lakes and farmland, and in 1970 it became the first new town in the country to receive federal financial help under Title IV of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1968.

The development corporation folded in 1979 and Chaska annexed the area. Today the Jonathan Association says the community has about 8,000 people on about 2,200 acres, bordering Hazeltine National Golf Club, the Chaska Town Course and the University of Minnesota Landscape Arboretum.

Many Jonathan homes use wood or vinyl siding and a modernist look, and trails run behind the houses past parks, ponds and marsh. The Association maintains more than 10 miles of these trails.

None of that tells us what is inside a particular wall, so we do not guess. We look at your system and tell you what we find.

What Home Age Means for Plumbing

The City of Chaska's 2040 plan says the Jonathan Development Corporation installed sewer and water here in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Neighborhoods were added over the following years, so homes in Jonathan span several decades.

Parts of a plumbing system wear out on a schedule no matter who built the house. A tank water heater often lasts 8 to 12 years, and supply valves, fixtures and drain lines age at different speeds.

Hard Water, in Plain Terms

The City of Chaska's 2025 water quality report lists its water at 26 grains per gallon of hardness, which is very hard. The city draws from seven wells between 333 and 817 feet deep and removes iron and manganese, but it does not soften the water.

Hard water leaves scale on heating elements, faucet screens and shower heads. A water heater that runs with scale buildup works harder, and a softener or regular flushing can help.

Trails, tot lots and exterior changes

Jonathan has sub-associations with their own by-laws, and the Association publishes ARC guidelines for residents. If your plumbing job touches anything outside the house, such as a new hose bib, a vent or visible equipment, check those documents first.

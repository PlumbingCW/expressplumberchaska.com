Lake Properties and Seasonal Systems

Homes on Lake Waconia and nearby lakes deal with a higher water table, seasonal occupancy and boat-season demand on the plumbing. A sump pit that rarely runs in a dry year can run constantly in a wet spring.

We test pumps and backups, check water heaters before the lake season and winterize systems for the months a property sits empty. See sump pump repair for what we check.

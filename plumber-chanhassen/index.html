Winter Protection for Chanhassen Homes

Lake-area homes, walkout basements and second-home properties are the ones that freeze. If you leave town in winter, keep the heat above 55 degrees, open cabinet doors on exterior walls and know where your main shutoff is.

Disconnect hoses from outdoor bibs before the first hard freeze. A hose left on traps water in the bib and splits the pipe behind the wall, which shows up as a leak in spring.

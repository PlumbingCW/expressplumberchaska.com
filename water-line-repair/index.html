Emergency Water Line Repair in Chaska, MN

If you see water pooling in the yard, hear water running with every tap closed or the meter spins with the house off, close the main valve inside the house and call. A service line leak wastes water and can undermine soil near the foundation.

We repair and replace water service lines in Chaska, from a short leak repair to a full run from the meter to the house, and we coordinate locates and permits.

Service Line Leak: How It Shows Up

Wet patch in the yard

A soggy, sunken or unusually green strip over the line route.

Meter runs with the house off

Water leaving the meter but not reaching a fixture.

Sudden low pressure

A break between the meter and the house.

No water at all

A burst or frozen line.

First, close the main inside the house and check the meter. If it keeps turning, the leak is before the valve, on the service line. If it stops, the leak is inside the house.

Then call us. We confirm the leak, mark its path and plan the repair after the utilities are marked.

What a Service Line Repair Visit Looks Like

After the locate, we dig to expose the line at the suspected spot, cut out the damaged section and join new pipe with approved fittings. We pressure test it, backfill in layers and compact the soil.

In Minnesota, service lines must run below the frost line, which is several feet down. A shallow line, or one that enters the house through an unheated space, is a repeat freeze risk and we point it out before we close the trench.

Water Line Repair Cost

A spot repair of a service line commonly runs from about $500 to $2,500 in typical US pricing, and a full replacement from about $2,500 to $8,000 or more, depending on length and surface. What changes it:

Length and depthLonger, deeper lines mean more digging.
SurfaceDriveways, sidewalks and landscaping add restoration.
Pipe typeCopper and PEX service pipes differ in cost.
Locates and permitsUtility marking and City permits are part of the job.

Water line coverage varies. Some homeowners policies and utility add-on plans cover service line repair. Ask before you dig.

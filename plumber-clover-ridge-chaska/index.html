A Newer Neighborhood on Pioneer Trail

Clover Ridge is one of the newer neighborhoods in Chaska. The City's housing plan calls its homes neo-traditional, and the City's 2040 land use plan places it along Clover Ridge Drive and Pioneer Trail, with Clover Field Marketplace at Hundertmark Road and Clover Ridge Drive.

A 2003 city planning document for the area describes about 1,500 housing units and a new elementary school north of Engler Boulevard. Clover Field, one of the neighborhoods inside Clover Ridge, broke ground on its first stage in April 2007, and its homes were built with small lots, sidewalks and alleys.

Clover Ridge has since been added to the Jonathan Association, so some residents deal with the same Association that covers Jonathan.

We do not assume a problem because a neighborhood is newer. We look at the system and tell you what we find.

What Newer Homes Need Over Time

Homes built in the late 2000s are now reaching the age when some parts begin to wear out. Many first-generation tank water heaters are at or past the age where replacement is common.

Fixtures, supply valves and garbage disposals also age on their own schedule. Small leaks at a supply line or a shutoff valve are usually cheap to fix and expensive to ignore.

Water heater is aging

If the heater is past 10 years, rust at the base or a rumble when it heats can be a warning. See water heater replacement.

Faucet or toilet keeps leaking

A small drip wastes water and can damage a cabinet. See faucet repair and toilet repair.

White scale on fixtures

Chaska city water measured 26 grains per gallon in 2025, so scale is common. See softener and filtration.

Slow kitchen drain or disposal jam

Grease and food buildup is a common reason. See garbage disposal repair.

Alleys, Small Lots and Where Things Are

Clover Field and neighborhoods like it use alleys behind the houses, which means garages and utility connections can sit on the back side. If you need to find your main shutoff, check where the water line enters the house, often near the water heater or a utility room.

Knowing where the main shutoff is saves time in a leak. If you cannot find it, we can show you during a visit.

Scale on Newer Fixtures

Newer fixtures with fine screens and small valves show scale early. White crust on a faucet aerator or a slow shower head is usually mineral buildup, and soaking the part in vinegar often clears it.

If the scale returns within weeks, the water is leaving a lot of minerals behind. A softener sized to your household is the longer term answer, and we can walk you through the options.

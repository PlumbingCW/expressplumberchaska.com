Emergency Boiler Repair in Chaska, MN

A boiler that has stopped heating in a Minnesota winter is an emergency. Check that the thermostat is calling for heat, the power switch is on and the pressure gauge reads in the normal range. If you smell gas or a carbon monoxide detector sounds, leave and call 911.

We diagnose and repair residential boilers in Chaska, including no-heat calls, ignition faults, circulator pumps, leaks, low pressure and noisy systems.

No Heat: What to Check Before You Call

1

Thermostat

Confirm it is set to heat and above room temperature.

2

Power

Check the boiler switch and breaker.

3

Pressure

A cold boiler usually reads between 12 and 15 psi. Low pressure can lock it out.

4

Gas

If other gas appliances work, the supply is likely fine.

5

Safety

Never ignore gas smells or carbon monoxide alarms.

If the reset button is lit or the display shows an error code, write down the code. Do not press reset more than once, since repeated resets can flood the burner with gas.

Call us with the brand, model and code. We will tell you what the code usually means and plan a visit.

Common Boiler Faults

Ignition failure

A dirty flame sensor or electrode keeps the burner from lighting.

Low pressure

A leak or failed expansion tank.

Kettling or banging

Scale or sludge on the heat exchanger.

Leaks

From a pump seal, valve or the heat exchanger.

Radiators cold at top

Air trapped in the system needs bleeding.

Annual Service and Carbon Monoxide Safety

A yearly tune-up checks the burner, flame sensor, venting, pressure, expansion tank and safety controls. It catches small faults before the first cold night and keeps combustion clean.

Every home with a gas boiler should have working carbon monoxide detectors on each level and near sleeping areas. A detector alarm means leave the house and call 911, not reset and wait.

If your boiler also feeds in-floor or baseboard zones, tell us which zones are cold. A single cold zone points to a zone valve or a trapped air pocket, while a whole-house failure points to the boiler or its controls.

Boiler Repair Cost

Common boiler repairs commonly run from about $200 to $900 in typical US pricing, depending on the part. A heat exchanger failure or a very old boiler can mean replacement. What changes it:

PartsPumps, valves and ignition parts differ in cost.
Boiler ageParts for older boilers may be hard to find.
TypeCondensing boilers have more electronics.
After hoursCold-night calls may carry a surcharge, stated up front.

Boiler repairs from wear are not insurance claims. A sudden burst or leak that damages the house may be covered.

Heating a Chaska Home in Winter

Hydronic heat with a boiler, radiators or in-floor tubing is found in many Minnesota homes, and a failure in January can freeze plumbing within a day if the house cools. Keep the thermostat above 55 degrees and open cabinet doors on exterior walls.

If your boiler also feeds your hot water, see water heater repair. Burst pipes after a freeze are handled by burst pipe repair.

Boiler piping, relief valves and drains are installed under the Minnesota Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so repairs are done to that standard.

Source: General Minnesota winter heating practice.
